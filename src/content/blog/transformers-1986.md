I saw Transformers for the first time and it was insane. This wasn't the Michael Bay Transformers, this was the original 1986 animated movie. 

Afterwards there was one word that kept bouncing around in my head: relentless. From the opening scenes and the first battle, it's just non-stop robot carnage. The 80s metal score just adds to the whole experience as well.

I didn't watch the cartoons when I was a kid. I didn't make it to the US until 1990. By that point, there might have been a series on TV but I didn't get it on one of the seven or eight over-the-air broadcast channels that I had access to. I had seen the 2007(?) Michael Bay transformers when it came out and was familiar with Optimus Prime and Bumble Bee and the others, but I wasn't prepared for the mega-battles that the original Transformers had.

The autobots just keep getting attacked and destroyed, get rebuilt, and get attacked again. It's just an endless cycle of destruction. 

I saw it as part of the 40th anniversary theatrical release in the excellent Prince Charles Cinema in Soho. That place, man, is epic in its own right. 

Anyway, this would be a movie I'd see again just to be able to process what happened.

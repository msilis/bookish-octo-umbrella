I've been posting on LinkedIn since I started my career move from music to tech. I had an account before I moved to the UK (now some 12 years ago!) but never used it. I also didn't write much before starting to post there. Writing was something that I did in school and then thought I'd leave, and mostly did leave. 



One of the hindrances is feeling like I'd either have nothing to say or that nobody really wanted to know what I wanted to say.



That's too much to unpack in a first post so I'll stay clear of that. 



I've been a software developer/engineer/person for three years now. I work in a media company and get to spend my days speaking with intelligent colleagues and cramming my brain with new info.



I'm not really sure what I'll post here, but it might be stories from my job search, why I wanted to change, what I found on the other side. 



It also seems nicer to post here rather than the Linky place. I've got nothing to sell and just want to tell my story. 



Ok, let's see how this goes.

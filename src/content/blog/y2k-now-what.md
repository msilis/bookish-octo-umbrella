This week there's been some doom, there's been some gloom, and probably some other stuff. AI will either blow up and ruin the economy, or it will <a href="https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgwy1k42w4o" rel="noopener noreferrer">annihilate humanity</a>. Or maybe it will be just a tool some people use. It's a little all over the place right now. 

I was doing the dishes and decided to listen to some music, scrolling through Spotify I found some stuff from <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Crystal_Method" rel=noopener noreferrer> The Crystal Method</a>. This brought back some memories. I grew up in the late 90s hysteria around Y2K. I was a kid that was into computers, was interested in technology in general, and listened to electronic music (alongside my other two favourite genres: classical and rap). This in itself was a wild time to be alive. Heading into the start of a new millennium as I myself was heading into adulthood. There was doom and gloom then too. Because of a small issue with computers and dates, we (well, not me personally) were stockpiling food, keeping cash around, and doing other kinds of nonsense. 

I remember my school had stickers on the computers which said whether they were Y2K compliant not. There was this thing that, as kids of that time, had grown up with that also might wreak untold havoc on us. This isn't on the scale of what people are talking about now but it felt huge at the time. Humanity wasn't going to be wiped out when ATMs refused to dispense money, it would just be annoying. There was a plan though. That might be something that's quite different from the proclamations coming out before and sometimes after new models are released. If someone does have plan now, they aren't in the mood to share. 

Whichever scenario plays out, we lose. We lose for a while and recover, or... just... kind of end. I myself am sceptical because it's what keeps me sane. There is so much more to the headlines, but it's hard to filter out all the crazy.

I like this article, <a href="https://thebulletin.org/2026/09/rogue-ai-didnt-breach-hugging-face-human-decisions-did/">Rogue AI didn’t breach Hugging Face, human decisions did</a> which goes beyond the headline of a few weeks ago and discusses in more detail what happened in the OpenAI/Hugging Face situation. I like the title though. I think that sums up quite a bit of the situation we're in. LLMs aren't making decisions and taking over stuff, it's just humans are being humans. 

Back to where I started with this, the music. I had a whole flood of memories come back of driving the Michigan country roads at night listening to Orbital, Leftfield, Chemical Brothers and others when I was in an electronic mood. Then Smashing Pumpkins, Radiohead, Hum and more when I needed some guitars and distortion in my life. Windows open in my VW Golf, night air rushing around my head, music blasting out of my tiny speakers. Man, that was a feeling.

Well, it was a cool trip down memory lane today, thanks brain.
